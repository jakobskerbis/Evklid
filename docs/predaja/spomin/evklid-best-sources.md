j 4 Nov 2026 11:08 (atlas thread): »Vedno ko potrebuješ vir Poišči najboljši možni.«

**Why:** j wants class-leading quality (atlas must beat Astra, lab must be realistic); a "good enough" source caps the result.

**How to apply:** before using a source, compare several candidates and pick the most accurate/detailed/authoritative one whose license allows commercial use (open licenses only: CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA; no NC/ND). Say in the PR/viri.json why it was chosen. Examples for 3D anatomy: CC0 MRI pial surfaces (OpenNeuro) for the brain cortex beat Z-Anatomy; also check HuBMAP Human Reference Atlas organs (CC BY 4.0) and CT segmentation datasets (TotalSegmentator, CC BY 4.0) per organ. For lab/lessons: primary literature, official tables (CRC, NIST, SURS, RIC) over blogs.
