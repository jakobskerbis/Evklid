## Target look (j 4 Nov 19:43: »Naj bodo modeli v tem stilu, vendar veliko bolj dodelani«)

Style reference: 6 Astra screenshots (plant, animal, bacterial cell and part cards) in /mnt/project-files/evklid/3d-atlas/slog-vzor/. NOT the HIV screenshot (that is our current model; j »Hiv ne«). Use as style only, never copy shapes or art. What to take: rich saturated colours with clear contrast between parts; sculpted organic surfaces (painted relief, fine hairs/pili, granular cytoplasm); cutaway views as the default; dense detail (hundreds to thousands of ribosomes, vesicles, cristae, ER folds via GPU instancing so phones stay smooth). What to do better than Astra: more structures, correct proportions and scale, thick membranes with solid cut edges, the baked ray-traced lighting of step 1b, labels that never cover the model (step 0b). Applies to every model; cells get the most change (step 6), organs and micro sections take the colour and detail rules in steps 1–2.

More references from j (19:49, same folder; style only, these are copyrighted renders): **celica-c4d-cela.png / celica-c4d-blizu.png** (Cinema 4D + Redshift animal cell): glassy translucent membrane with rim light, deep purple nucleus with cut, layered blue ER studded with gold ribosomes, gold mitochondria, yellow cytoskeleton fibres, dark violet gradient background. This is the main target for cells. On phones fake the glass (fresnel + transparency) instead of real transmission. **celica-molekulska-gostota.png** (molecular-scale cell): crowded cytoplasm with proteins, filaments, vesicles; target for a new deepest zoom level (step 6b). **Heart (j 19:53): srce-prerez-1…5.png** (cross-section video stills): glossy wet pink myocardium with fine fibre ridges on the cut, trabeculae carneae, papillary muscles with white chordae tendineae to the valve leaflets, translucent cream valve leaflets, coronary arteries red and veins blue on the surface, black background. **srce-zunaj.png** (outside): deep red muscle with yellowish epicardial fat along the grooves, branching coronary vessels, wet sheen. Target for the heart in step 1 (and the organ look in general). Heart needs real chordae, papillary muscles and trabeculae geometry if the source mesh lacks them (+4–6 $ in step 1).
