  ct_orodja.py, pljuca_geometrija.py.

  Trachea v1.1 in Main Bronchus v1.1 (Visible Human Male), https://humanatlas.io/3d-reference-library, CC BY 4.0.
  Prirejeno (afina poravnava na dihalno drevo CT, stena, poenostavitev).
