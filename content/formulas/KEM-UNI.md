|---|---|
| Entalpija | $\Delta H = Q_p$, $\; \Delta_r H^\circ = \sum_{\text{prod.}} \nu\,\Delta_f H^\circ - \sum_{\text{reakt.}} \nu\,\Delta_f H^\circ$ |

| Toplota, kalorimetrija | $Q = m c \Delta T = C \Delta T$ |
